# Myth & Machine Labs

Free, hands-on practice projects for the **Myth & Machine** YouTube tech education series.
Each lesson has a short lab you can do in your browser, plus a quiz to check yourself.

## Paths

| Path | Where it leads | Lessons |
|---|---|---|
| **From Switch to Silicon** | Electronics Technician, then Embedded Systems or GPU/AI Hardware Engineer (with later paths) | [Lesson 1: What is electricity?](from-switch-to-silicon/lesson-01/) |

More paths (Cloud/DevOps, AI) are coming.

## How to use
1. Fork this repo (button at the top right).
2. Open a lesson folder and follow its README.
3. Write your answers in your fork and compare with the answer section.

Watch the lessons on YouTube: **Myth & Machine** (@sathishramalingam8).
